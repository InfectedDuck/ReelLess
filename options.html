    Optional commitment

    Ultimate Lock

    Lock protection to remove ReelLess pause and setting changes. Choose what stays blocked before you turn it on.

    This forces protection on and disables pausing, platform switches, schedules, and custom-list changes in ReelLess. Removing it requires a private reflection, three active check-ins, and at least one focused minute.

    It cannot stop Chrome from disabling, uninstalling, or clearing the extension.

    Ultimate Lock is active. Protection is on and the selected profile is fixed.
