Core protection

Four sites, one window. Pick Short-form only, Selected sections, or Block everything per site.

Ready after install

Turn a site off with its Protected switch — turning it back on restores your previous mode and choices. Choose Selected sections to tick exactly which parts to block — e.g. Home feed, Explore, Stories. Blocking the YouTube Home page stops the whole page with a focus screen; hiding Home recommendations only quiets the feed under “Hide page elements”. For each site, also choose whether blocked Shorts and Reels are hidden from feeds or stay visible but cannot be opened. Your choices stay in this browser.

Custom pages

Add a personal boundary

Block any distracting page the checklists above do not cover, e.g. example.com/shorts or example.com/explore. Matching is exact-host (www.example.com and example.com are different — add each separately) and case-sensitive for paths. Direct visits may show the browser's blocked-page error; navigation within guarded pages uses the ReelLess focus screen. Access is requested only for the entry you add.

    Ultimate Lock

    Lock protection to remove ReelLess pause and setting changes. Choose what stays blocked before you turn it on.

    This forces protection on and disables pausing, platform switches, schedules, and custom-list changes in ReelLess. Removing it requires a private reflection, three active check-ins, and at least one focused minute.

    It cannot stop the browser from disabling, uninstalling, or clearing the extension.

    Ultimate Lock is active. Protection is on and the selected profile is fixed.

    Coverage

    What ReelLess covers

    Four default sites (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) plus seven optional sites (X, Reddit, Snapchat, Twitch, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Threads). Facebook protection is Reels and videos: feed videos, Watch, Live, and video posts are included with Reels — uncheck Watch under Selected sections to allow videos again. Preserved: YouTube watch pages and search, Instagram profiles and messages, Facebook groups/messages/marketplace (unless blocked), TikTok messages/upload/settings, and all Direct/Messenger conversations. Desktop Chrome, Edge, and Firefox are supported. This affects supported browser pages, not native social apps or every embedded player.

    Local data

    Stored on this device only

    Settings and Total blocked attempts stay in browser storage. Uninstalling the extension or clearing browser data removes them. Resetting counters leaves settings, lock, and review state untouched.
